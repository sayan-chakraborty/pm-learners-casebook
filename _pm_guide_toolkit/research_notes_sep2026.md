## Learning-science basis
- Worked examples (Sweller/Renkl); benefit persists in ill-structured domains (legal-case study). Fade: full example → completion problem → full problem (expertise reversal).
- Contrasting cases before telling: transfer 51% vs 23% (Schwartz et al. 2011). Comparing two cases: 48% vs 19% transfer (Gentner, Loewenstein & Thompson 2003).
- Productive failure meta-analysis: g = 0.36 conceptual/transfer; ~0 procedural (Sinha & Kapur 2021).
- Retrieval practice and spacing = only "high utility" techniques; interleaving moderate; rereading/highlighting/summarising low (Dunlosky 2013).
- Erroneous examples: delayed-test advantage d = .33 (McLaren et al.).
- Concreteness fading: concrete → abstract (Fyfe et al. 2014).

## Key PM resources (web)
- Lenny's "Definitive guide to product sense interviews": 5 graded dimensions — communication (game plan, waypointing), product motivation (mission), segmentation (ecosystem → behavioural segments → reach vs underserved), problem identification (journey; needs ≠ problems; severity × frequency), solutions (3 distinct ideas, impact/effort, concrete v1, risks). Must pass every dimension.
- Exponent 2026 study plan: 5 rounds — product sense, analytical/execution, strategy, behavioural, technical/AI fluency. 2026 shifts: conflicting-metric questions, 3–5 follow-up layers on behavioural stories, company-specific prompts, AI rounds.
- Aakash Gupta AI PM guide: model-layer vs application-layer problems; quantified estimates; safety woven in; evals, latency/accuracy trade-offs.
- Shreyas Doshi: LNO (Leverage/Neutral/Overhead), 3 levels (impact/execution/optics), pre-mortems, opportunity cost > ROI, most execution problems are strategy problems.
- Marty Cagan: 4 risks — value, usability, feasibility, viability.
- Teresa Torres: Opportunity Solution Tree (outcome → opportunities → solutions → experiments).
- Gibson Biddle: DHM (Delight, Hard-to-copy, Margin-enhancing).
- Ben Thompson: Aggregation theory. Hamilton Helmer: 7 Powers (scale economies, network economies, counter-positioning, switching costs, branding, cornered resource, process power).
- Andrew Chen: Cold Start Problem / atomic network. Reforge: growth loops > funnels. Sean Ellis 40% PMF test.
- Lenny retention benchmarks (6-month user retention, good/great): consumer social 25/45%, consumer transactional 30/50%, consumer subscription 40/70%, SMB SaaS 60/80%, enterprise SaaS 70/90%.
