MakeMyTrip: the flight brings you in, the hotel pays the bills

Walkthrough (illustrative persona). Rohit, 30, plans a December weekend in Goa with his wife. MakeMyTrip compares over 30 flights in seconds; he picks an IndiGo flight at ₹5,400 each and pays ₹11,200 including a convenience fee. The ticket is issued and a PNR appears. The next screen asks: “Add a hotel?” He books two nights at ₹8,000. The flight earned MakeMyTrip about ₹400; the hotel, about ₹1,400 ({{REF:mm-walk}}).

Core loop and business model

Flights are the most searched travel product and the least profitable. They bring people to the app often; each visit is a chance to sell a hotel, a package or a bus ticket, which pay more. More customers attract more hotels, which want to be listed where the travellers are, which gives customers more choice. In FY26 (to March 2026), MakeMyTrip handled $10.4 bn of bookings (+10.4% in constant currency), passed $1 bn of revenue for the first time and earned an operating profit of $156 mn. In April–June 2026, bookings grew 19.9% to $2.85 bn, though international travel was subdued by the West Asia conflict. The fastest-growing lines are the non-flight ones ({{REF:mm-seg}}).

Moat, in plain words. Relationships with tens of thousands of Indian hotels and homestays; redBus, the leader in online bus tickets; a brand that most Indian travellers try first; and corporate accounts through myBiz. Airlines can sell direct, hotels much less so.

2025–26 moves. An experiences-booking platform (tours and activities, 2025); more AI trip planning in the app; growth in the UAE business for residents, including Umrah packages.

Strengths

  • Largest Indian hotel supply
  • redBus and myBiz widen the base
  • Profitable and growing

Weaknesses

  • Airlines push travellers to book direct
  • Google and AI assistants can intercept the search
  • International travel is exposed to shocks

What I’d build next

1. Flyers book the hotel elsewhere → a “trip pack” price that drops when flight and hotel are booked together within 24 hours → hotels per flight booking.

2. Refunds after cancellations take weeks → instant refund to UPI for low-risk bookings, recovered from the airline later → repeat bookings within 90 days; complaints.

3. Indians in the Gulf fly home often → a “going home” hub: baggage-clear fares, festival alerts, family packages → repeat UAE customers.
