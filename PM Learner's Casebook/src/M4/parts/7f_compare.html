In the interview · How these products come up
  • “Why did Ola lose to Uber and Rapido?” Drivers first: payout trust fell, drivers left, waits grew, riders followed. A two-sided market shrinks as fast as it grows.
  • “Is Rapido’s subscription model sustainable?” It wins drivers and cheap fares, but revenue doesn’t grow with rides, so it needs volume products: autos, cabs, parcels, food.
  • “How does Google Maps make money?” Local ads when people search nearby, fees from apps that use its maps, and more valuable ads in Search.
  • “Why do travel apps push hotels?” A flight earns a few hundred rupees; a hotel pays 15–20%.
