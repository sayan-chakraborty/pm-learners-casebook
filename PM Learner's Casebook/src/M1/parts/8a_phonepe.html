PhonePe: free payments, paid loans

Walkthrough (illustrative persona). Meena, 41, runs a tailoring shop in Indore. On the 5th of every month she opens PhonePe, taps Power, and the app fetches her ₹1,240 electricity bill from her consumer number. One PIN later it’s paid, and the success screen offers her a business loan. Three screens show the whole business model: a free, frequent job brings her back, and the money is made on what she sees next.

Business model in one line. Payments bring people in at a loss; revenue comes from merchant devices, bill payments, a small government incentive for UPI, and increasingly from distributing loans, insurance and investments. The lending arm alone grew from ₹378 Cr to ₹945 Cr in a year.

North StarMonthly transacting users: at least one successful payment in 30 days.
InputsNew P2P senders · live merchant QRs · payment success rate · loans per 1,000 users.
GuardrailsFraud loss per ₹1 lakh moved · reversal complaints · loans 30+ days overdue.

Moat, in plain words. Habit and reach: 45.9% of UPI payments start on PhonePe (Aug 2026), and its QR codes and speakers sit in lakhs of shops. The crack in the wall: UPI makes switching free, because any UPI app scans any QR. So the moat is only as deep as the reasons to open PhonePe first: bills, loans, rewards, trust.

2025–26 moves. Pincode, its quick-commerce app, wound down (Dec 2025). SEBI cleared its IPO (Jan 2026); listing paused on market volatility (Mar 2026). Indus Appstore written down in FY26. Merchant lending platform and SmartPOD (speaker plus card machine). Coming: 0.4% MDR on large merchants (15 Oct 2026) and the 30% cap deadline (31 Dec 2026).

Strengths

  • Largest UPI share, and the default app for Bharat’s shopkeepers
  • Lending revenue growing 2.5× a year, on payment data rivals lack
  • Trusted brand among first-time digital users

Weaknesses

  • Loss-making (−₹2,792 Cr in FY26); payments earn about nothing
  • Growth could be frozen by the 30% cap
  • Diversification misfires: Pincode shut, Indus Appstore written down

What I’d build next

1. A pending payment at a counter breaks trust on both sides → the speaker says “payment pending, PhonePe guarantees it” for pending payments under ₹500 → repeat payments at that shop within 7 days.

2. Small merchants pay no MDR, so they earn PhonePe nothing → use speaker takings as the credit signal for working-capital loans → approval rate and 30+ day defaults, speaker merchants vs others.

3. Large merchants like the Surat trader will push buyers to personal UPI IDs to dodge the new fee → a fee dashboard that shows the 0.4% next to the value of instant settlement, GST-ready receipts and credit built on business payments → share of large merchants keeping payments on their business QR after 15 October.
