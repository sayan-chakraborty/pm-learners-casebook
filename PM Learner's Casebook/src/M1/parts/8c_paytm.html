Paytm, and the super-app question

Walkthrough (illustrative persona). Sunita, 45, runs a tea stall outside Lucknow’s Charbagh station. Her Paytm Soundbox calls out every payment, including the ₹10 ones, so she never checks a customer’s phone. At night the day’s ₹6,850 lands in her SBI account, and the app offers her a ₹50,000 loan repaid in small daily amounts. Paytm lost most of its consumer lead to PhonePe and Google Pay, then rebuilt around shopkeepers like Sunita.

Check · “Paytm is India’s super-app” Overstated

Paytm tried: shopping, games, travel, movies and a bank, all in one app. Three things worked against it. In India, messaging belongs to WhatsApp, so no payments app owns the daily social habit that carried WeChat Pay. Android and the Play Store make standalone apps easy to install, so mini-apps save users little. And UPI lets every app pay every shop, so payments can’t lock users in. Paytm today is a merchant-payments and financial-distribution company with some consumer services, and its results improved when it narrowed.

North StarMerchants on a paid device subscription who transact daily.
InputsDevices deployed · merchant payment volume (GMV) · loans distributed per 100 merchants.
GuardrailsDevice churn · loan collection rate · compliance findings.

Moat, in plain words. A field force and 1.51 crore merchants paying for devices: switching means returning a device and retraining staff, which a UPI app can’t replicate with software alone. 2025–26 moves: first full year of profit (FY26); RBI cancelled the Payments Bank licence (24 Apr 2026), ending a two-year overhang; Paytm Intelligence, AI agents for banks and insurers (Sep 2026).

Strengths

  • Earns from merchants: devices, card fees, loan distribution
  • Profitable, with revenue growing faster than costs
  • Deep merchant data for credit and fraud models

Weaknesses

  • Consumer UPI share fell to 8.1% after the 2024 curbs
  • Regulatory history makes RBI and partners cautious
  • AI pivot is new and unproven as a business

What I’d build next

1. Soundbox merchants borrow informally at high rates when stock runs out → a small credit line that grows with daily takings, drawn by voice on the Soundbox (“bolo, ₹5,000 chahiye”) → credit-line use and 30+ day defaults per 100 merchants.

2. Shopkeepers can’t read dashboards while serving customers → the Soundbox reads out the day’s total at closing time in their language → weekly active merchants on the app, and device churn.
