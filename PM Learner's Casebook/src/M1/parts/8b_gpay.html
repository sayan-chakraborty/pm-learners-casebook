Google Pay: the payment app that lives inside Google

Walkthrough (illustrative persona). Rahul, 24, a software tester in Bengaluru, owes his flatmate Aman ₹250 for a cab. He opens Google Pay, taps Aman’s face, sees their payment history as a chat, pays, and scratches a card that gives him ₹11 back and a Lenskart coupon. Google designed the whole flow to feel like messaging a friend, not visiting a bank.

Business model in one line. Google Pay India’s own revenue is small (₹1,547 Cr in FY25, profit ₹50 Cr) and flat for five years. It earns from loan and card distribution, partner-funded offers and fees on card bill payments (0.5–1% plus GST when paid by card; free from a bank account by UPI). Its bigger value is strategic: payments are the most frequent reason to open a Google app in India.

North StarMonthly payers making 10+ payments: a habit measure, not a download count.
InputsP2P pairs · payment success rate · reward redemption · Flex cards issued.
GuardrailsScam reports per lakh payments · reward cost per payment · overdue card balances.

Moat, in plain words. It comes pre-installed in trust: a Google account, Android, and a clean interface people already know. Chat threads make leaving costly in a small way, because your payment history with each friend lives there. Its fraud tools draw on Google’s scale. 2025–26 moves: Flex RuPay credit card with Axis Bank and Pocket Money on UPI Circle (both Dec 2025); screen-sharing scam alerts; merchant ratings synced to Google Maps; convenience fees on card-funded bill payments.

Strengths

  • 32.4% of UPI; the cleanest, most social interface
  • Google’s trust and scale in fraud detection
  • Rewards partly funded by partner brands

Weaknesses

  • Small Indian revenue for its size; every financial product depends on partners
  • Weaker with offline merchants than PhonePe and Paytm
  • Also above the 30% cap, if it is ever enforced

What I’d build next

1. Group expenses (flats, trips) are tracked in spreadsheets and chased by message → shared groups with running balances and one-tap “settle up” by UPI → active groups settling at least monthly.

2. Most scams happen while the victim is on a call with the scammer → with permission, warn when a payment to a new payee starts during a call from an unsaved number → scam reports per lakh payments, and genuine payments abandoned after the warning.

3. Flex cardholders are new to credit and may overspend → a weekly “what you owe” nudge in the chat-style home, with autopay from the bank account → share of card bills paid in full on time.
