In the interview · “Which of these would you rather be PM of?”

Pick one and argue from the business model, not the app’s looks: “Paytm. It is the only one here earning real money from merchants rather than from the payment itself, it has just turned profitable, and the next product decision matters: whether merchant credit or the new AI business gets the best engineers. That’s a PM’s job with real trade-offs.” Then name the risk you’d watch.

Key idea · Read any fintech in three numbers

Share (how many use it) · revenue per user (what the habit earns) · loss or default rate (what it costs). PhonePe leads the first, Zerodha the second, and every lender lives or dies by the third.
