In the interview · How these products come up
  • “Why is Myntra profitable when Flipkart isn’t?” One category, loyal young buyers who browse often, logistics fees from sellers and fast-growing ads; Flipkart funds a price war and a speed war at once.
  • “Should Amazon worry about quick commerce?” Yes, for frequency: the everyday ₹200 orders that keep people opening the app. Not much for big, planned purchases. That is why Amazon Now exists.
  • “How does Google make money if AI answers the question?” Most revenue comes from the small share of searches where people want to buy; AI answers mostly replace the informational ones. Watch clicks to advertisers and publishers.
  • “Explain real-time bidding to my grandmother.” Every time a free app shows an ad, a tiny auction decides which advertiser pays most to show it to you, and it finishes before the page loads.
