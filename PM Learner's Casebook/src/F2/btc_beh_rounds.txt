QUESTION BANK The following are sample questions you can practice in groups. Brainstorm ideas, approaches, and frameworks with your peers. 1. PRODUCT DESIGN B2C a. Consumer Software 1. Design a product that helps gig workers manage their personal finance. 2. Design a digital product for people who regularly commute between two cities for work. Focus on one high-frequency problem rather than attempting to solve the entire commuting journey. 3. Design a product that helps consumers make better decisions when purchasing refurbished electronics. 4. Design a hyperlocal news app for rural India. 5. Design a product that helps users reduce unnecessary online shopping. Assume the business still wants users to remain engaged with the platform. 6. Design a digital experience for attending a large live event such as a concert or sporting event. 7. Design a product that helps elderly users independently manage routine digital tasks such as payments, bookings, and appointments. 8. Design a product for households that want to reduce their electricity consumption without significantly changing their lifestyle. Identify the highest-value intervention and explain the behavioural challenge you are solving. b. Physical Products/IoT 9. Design an intelligent shoe for visually impaired users. B2B 10. Design a product that helps small retailers understand which products are likely to run out of stock in the coming week. 11. Design a product that helps sales representatives prepare for their customer meetings. Decide which part of the sales workflow you would solve first. 12. Design a product for independent restaurants to manage their supplier orders. 13. Design a product for independent healthcare clinics to reduce the administrative work involved in managing appointments and follow-ups. 14. Design an AI copilot for procurement teams that helps them evaluate supplier proposals. Explain what decisions the AI should assist with and where human approval should remain mandatory. 15. A logistics company wants to give its enterprise customers greater visibility into shipment disruptions. Design the product. 16. Design a platform that enables construction companies to coordinate multiple subcontractors working on the same project. 2. PRODUCT IMPROVEMENT B2C a. Feature Enhancement 17. Improve the experience of saving and organizing articles, videos, and posts that users discover online. Assume users frequently save content but rarely return to it. 18. A grocery delivery app has strong order frequency but users increasingly complain that they cannot find what they want. How would you improve the discovery experience? 19. Improve a travel-booking platform for users who are still exploring destinations and have not decided where they want to travel. b. UX/Accessibility 20. Improve the experience of buying tickets for popular events where inventory sells out quickly. c. Performance Adoption 21. A short-form video platform wants to increase meaningful engagement without increasing average screen time. How would you rethink the product? 22. An online learning product has strong course completion rates but very low conversion from learning to real-world application. How would you improve the product? 23. Improve an AI-powered consumer assistant whose usage is high initially but falls sharply after the first few weeks. Identify why this might happen and what you would change. B2B 24. Improve an expense-management platform where employees submit expenses regularly but finance teams still spend significant time correcting submissions. 25. A B2B analytics platform has excellent functionality but new customers take three months to become productive. How would you improve the onboarding experience? 26. Improve a project-management product used by consulting teams where senior leaders want visibility but consultants complain about excessive reporting requirements.
