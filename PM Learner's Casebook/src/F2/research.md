## Ideas used (rewritten, ISB BTC Handbook pp. 39–44)
spoken vs written answers; fit vs how you operate; TMAY ~2 min explaining choices; Why/What/How/Impact/Future scope for projects; 5–7 core stories; story as bullet memories (context, role, decisions, actions, stakeholders, result, reflection); stress tests are a format, don't invent a better story under pressure.
