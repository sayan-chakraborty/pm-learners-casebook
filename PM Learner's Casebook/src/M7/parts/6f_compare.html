In the interview · How these products come up
  • “Why does Duolingo keep people and fitness apps don’t?” Tiny daily task, a streak that hurts to lose, and a prompt at the right time; fitness asks for a big effort and gives slow results.
  • “How would you make a coaching business more profitable?” Raise clients per coach with AI drafts the coach checks, and guard renewals and outcomes while doing it.
  • “Why did PhysicsWallah succeed where Byju’s failed?” Free and cheap first, trust in teachers, classrooms as the premium; Byju’s sold expensive courses on loans.
  • “Design a feature for Practo.” Start from the doctor’s side as well as the patient’s: the slot, the records, the follow-up.
