HealthifyMe: counting calories so a coach can do the rest

Walkthrough (illustrative persona). Neha, back in Indore, restarts in March with a coaching plan, because a wedding is six weeks away. At lunch she photographs her thali. The app guesses dal, two rotis, rice and sabzi, about 640 kcal, and shows each portion. She changes the rotis to one. Her day now shows 1,420 of 1,600 kcal. A tip appears: she is low on protein, so curd at dinner would help. On Saturday her dietitian, who has her whole week in front of her, calls for ten minutes and plans the wedding week: what to eat at the sangeet and how to get back on track after ({{REF:hm-flow}}).

Core loop and business model

The daily loop is log → see the day’s total → get a tip → log the next meal. The paid loop is weekly: the coach reviews the log, the user feels watched in a good way, and renews. The business is a ladder ({{REF:hm-ladder}}): free tracking brings people in; each step up adds more expert time and costs more. HealthifyMe’s revenue fell 14% to ₹178 crore in FY25, with coaching services at ₹99 crore, but it cut its loss by 96% to ₹4.7 crore by spending less on ads. Its bet now is weight-loss drugs: people on GLP-1 medicines need diet and muscle guidance, and coaches plus doctors can charge far more than a calorie counter.

Moat, in plain words. A large database of Indian dishes and years of meal photos make the photo AI better on Indian food than a global app; a coach who knows you is hard to leave mid-plan.

North StarPaying users with healthy progress towards their goal each month (for example, 0.5–1 kg a week).

Strengths

  • Best Indian food data
  • Human coaches people trust
  • Near break-even

Weaknesses

  • Shrinking revenue in India
  • Free rivals for tracking (phones, watches)
  • Coach time limits margins

What I’d build next

1. New users quit before their first meal log → five questions, then “snap your lunch” as the first screen → share with 3 meals logged in 3 days.

2. Coaches spend time writing the same feedback → AI drafts each client’s weekly note, the coach edits and sends → clients per coach, with renewal rate as the guardrail.

3. People on weight-loss drugs lose muscle → a protein and strength plan linked to the dose schedule, reviewed by the prescribing doctor → muscle mass kept (smart-scale readings); plan renewals.
