In the interview · How these products come up
  • “Why doesn’t Netflix show IPL?” Rights cost about ₹58 crore a match for digital alone; that only pays back with a mass ad business, which Netflix doesn’t run in India.
  • “How does Spotify make money if most users are free?” Free listeners see ads and many later upgrade; Premium brings most revenue; labels take about two-thirds.
  • “Why does Prime Video exist?” To make Prime renew and keep people shopping on Amazon; its ads now use shopping data.
  • “Design a monetisation feature for a free game.” Rewarded ads for non-payers, small packs at the near-miss moment, and a guardrail on heavy spenders.
