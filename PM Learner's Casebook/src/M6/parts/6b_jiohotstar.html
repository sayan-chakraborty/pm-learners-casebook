JioHotstar: cricket for everyone, paid for by ads

Walkthrough. Back to Priya on the hostel steps in Ahmedabad on final night. She pays ₹79 a month for the phone plan, but most of her friends watch on a plan bundled with their Jio recharge. She picks Hindi commentary. The picture starts blurry and sharpens. At the end of the over, two 10-second ads play: a phone brand and a biscuit brand, in Hindi. Her friend in Chennai, watching the same ball, sees two different ads in Tamil. Nobody chose those ads by hand: a computer picked them for each of the crores of phones watching ({{REF:jh-flow}}).

Business model, with numbers. JioStar earns from ads (sport above all), subscriptions (many through recharges) and TV channels. In April–June 2026, the IPL quarter, its revenue was ₹10,946 crore, up 14%, and JioHotstar averaged 53 crore monthly users. Prices rose in January 2026: phone ₹79 a month, Super ₹149, Premium ₹299. The biggest cost is rights: IPL, ICC events and studio libraries, paid for whether or not the ads sell.

Moat, in plain words. Almost all top cricket in one app; Jio’s hundreds of millions of phone customers as a sales channel; and the engineering and ad-sales machine to serve crores at once. The risk is the same as the moat: rights cost a fortune, and a weak ad year doesn’t lower them.

Prime Video earns in a different way. For most Indians it comes inside Amazon Prime (₹1,499 a year), which also gives free, fast delivery. Its job is to make Prime feel worth renewing, so that members keep shopping on Amazon ({{REF:pv-loop}}). Since 17 June 2025 it shows ads by default in India; removing them costs ₹129 a month. Its pitch to advertisers is not JioHotstar’s mass reach but ads matched to real shopping data, with sales measured on Amazon. For free viewers, Amazon runs Amazon MX Player, with ads.

Strengths

  • Owns India’s biggest live events
  • Every language, every price point
  • Proven at record scale

Weaknesses

  • Two months of peak a year
  • Rights costs fixed, ad revenue not
  • Many users never chose to pay

What I’d build next

1. Lakhs leave after the IPL → pause instead of cancel, and an “after the IPL” watchlist built during the season → share of IPL joiners watching weekly 60 days after the final.

2. Advertisers pay for reach but want sales → tap an ad during a strategic time-out to save the offer to WhatsApp or JioMart, paid by UPI later → revenue per 1,000 ad views; offer saves per ad.
