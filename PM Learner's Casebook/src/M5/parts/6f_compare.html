In the interview · How these products come up
  • “How does Meta make money from WhatsApp?” Business messages by type, click-to-WhatsApp ads, and the Updates tab. Never ads in chats.
  • “Why did Instagram beat Snapchat?” It copied Stories in 2016 and gave them to a much bigger network; the features were similar, the audience wasn’t.
  • “Why do small shops love Meta ads?” ₹500 a day, targeting by distance, and a chat instead of a website.
  • “Is Community Notes a good design?” Yes: it needs agreement across sides, so neither side can outvote the other. Its weakness is speed: a note often arrives after a false post has spread.
