WhatsApp: India’s default way to talk, and now to buy

Walkthrough (illustrative persona). Neha, 31, in Jaipur, is watching Reels when an ad from a Sanganeri saree shop shows a “Send message” button. She taps it and WhatsApp opens a chat with the shop, with “Hi, I’m interested” already typed. The shop’s AI assistant replies in Hindi within seconds: three colours, ₹2,400, delivery in four days. She picks one from the catalogue in the chat, pays by UPI without leaving WhatsApp, and gets an “order shipped” message two days later. Meta earned twice: once for the ad, and a few paise for the shipping update ({{REF:wa-flow}}).

How WhatsApp earns without ads in chats

Users pay nothing. The money comes from three places. Business messages, priced per message by type ({{REF:wa-price}}). Click-to-WhatsApp ads on Facebook and Instagram, which open a chat instead of a website. And since 2025, ads and paid subscriptions in the Updates tab, where Status and Channels live, never in personal chats. Meta’s “other revenue”, mostly business messaging, reached $1 bn in April–June 2026, up 73%, and more than 10 lakh businesses use its AI agents each week.

Moat, in plain words. Everyone you know is on it, reachable by the phone number already in your contacts, and years of chats and photos live in it. That makes it one of the strongest positions in consumer technology. The risks are misinformation spreading through forwards, pressure from governments to trace messages, and the temptation to earn so much from businesses that personal chats fill up with offers.

North StarDaily users who send a message or make a call.

2025–26 moves. Per-message pricing (July 2025) and rupee billing (January 2026). Ads and paid Channel subscriptions in the Updates tab. Business AI agents at scale. In June 2026 Kunal Shah, founder of CRED, was named head of WhatsApp, a sign that payments and commerce will get more attention.

Real-life example · Telegram, the public messenger

Telegram made the opposite choices, and each follows from one decision: it is built for people who don’t know each other. It has over 1 bn monthly users, about 15 mn paying Premium subscribers, and India was its biggest download market in 2025. It made its first annual profit, $547 mn, in 2024, and $870 mn of revenue in January–June 2025.

Strengths

  • Nearly every smartphone user in India
  • Private by default, and trusted for it
  • Businesses already talk to customers here

Weaknesses

  • Little revenue per user
  • Forwards spread rumours it can’t see
  • Weaker than Telegram for large public groups

What I’d build next

1. Offers from businesses crowd out personal chats → a “Businesses” folder with one-tap unsubscribe and a monthly cap per brand → business blocks per 1,000 messages (down); order-update read rate (guardrail: marketing revenue).

2. Kirana owners take orders in chat and lose track → turn any customer message into an order card with items, total and a UPI request → weekly active small businesses; payments started from chats.

3. Housing societies chase maintenance fees in groups → fee requests with UPI and a paid/unpaid list for the committee inside Communities → active society Communities; share of fees paid on time.
