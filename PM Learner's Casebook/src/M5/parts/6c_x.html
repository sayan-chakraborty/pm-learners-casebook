X: the fastest place for news, and a shrinking business

Walkthrough (illustrative persona). Meera, a reporter in Mumbai, sees smoke over a railway yard at 7:10 pm and posts a photo with two lines. Her 80,000 followers get it in their “Following” timeline within seconds. X’s “For You” ranking notices fast replies and reposts, and shows it to people in Mumbai who don’t follow her. Within an hour, another account posts an old video from a different fire, claiming it is tonight’s. Readers who add context write a note: “This video is from 2019.” The note appears under the post only after raters who usually disagree with each other both mark it helpful ({{REF:x-flow}}).

The clever part: notes that both sides must agree on

Most fact-check systems ask a few experts, and half the audience distrusts them. X’s Community Notes ask users instead, but a note isn’t shown just because many people like it; that would let the bigger side win every time. The system learns which raters usually disagree, and shows a note only when people from both groups rate it helpful ({{REF:bridge}}). Designers call this bridging. Meta copied the idea for Facebook, Instagram and Threads in the US in 2025.

Business model, with numbers

X earns from ads and from subscriptions (Premium, for a blue tick and more reach). After Elon Musk bought Twitter in October 2022 for $44 bn and cut about 80% of its staff, many big advertisers left. Ad revenue fell from $4.4 bn in 2022 to about $2 bn in 2025. X now sits inside the SpaceX group through its merger with xAI, and a 2026 filing showed 550 mn monthly users, fewer than the 600 mn X had claimed, and about 6.3 mn paying subscribers. It is also testing X Money, for payments.

Moat, in plain words. Journalists, politicians, investors and sports fans still post here first, so news breaks here first. That habit is hard to copy, but Threads and Bluesky are trying. In India, X is where governments and ministers announce things, which also makes it a frequent subject of takedown orders: X challenged India’s Sahyog portal for such orders in the Karnataka High Court and lost in September 2025.

Strengths

  • Real-time news and public debate
  • Community Notes, a design others copy
  • Grok AI built in

Weaknesses

  • Ad revenue half of 2022’s
  • Decisions depend heavily on one owner
  • Bots, spam and brand-safety worries

What I’d build next

1. During a crisis, people can’t tell rumours from reports → a “verified sources” view that shows only news organisations and posts with notes → daily users during breaking news; time from false post to note.

2. Indian cricket fans scatter across apps during matches → official match rooms with live scores and highlights in Hindi, Tamil and Bengali → match-day daily users; ad revenue per match.
